# Code of Conduct

We want `immulog` to be a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive
project for everyone who participates.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and considerate of different experiences and perspectives;
- discuss ideas and code directly without attacking people;
- give and receive actionable feedback in good faith;
- keep discussions focused on the project and its users; and
- take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing private information without consent;
- deliberate intimidation, threats, or sustained disruption;
- abusive conduct in issues, pull requests, reviews, or other project spaces; or
- any other conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe or
  unwelcome.

## Reporting concerns

Please report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through
GitHub rather than raising them in a public issue. Include enough context to
investigate, but avoid sharing private information or sensitive details more
widely than necessary.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where possible,
and respond with the action they consider appropriate. Reports made in good
faith will not result in retaliation.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issue and pull-request discussions,
reviews, and project-related community spaces. Maintainers may remove comments,
restrict participation, or take other action when behavior violates this policy.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant community
standard.
